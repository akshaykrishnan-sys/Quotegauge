How we price a repair, and where the estimate stops.

You should know what a number is built from before you rely on it.

What goes into each range

  • The standard labor time for that job on your exact vehicle
  • Typical hourly labor rates for your region and kind of shop
  • Typical parts cost, from economy to original-equipment quality
  • The usual maintenance schedule for your car and mileage

A line is marked above market when it is more than 10% over the top of its fair range.

What it can't know

  • The condition of your car, or what the technician found
  • The brand and grade of parts the shop plans to fit
  • Rust, seized bolts and other things that add real labor

That is why some lines come back as needs verification. QuoteGauge is an automated estimate, not an inspection, and never a claim that a shop acted dishonestly. Read the full method.
