When we refund

If you paid for a full report and it did not open, was blank, or plainly failed to read your estimate, email us within 7 days of purchase and we will refund what you paid for it.

When we don't

A report is delivered the moment it opens, so we do not refund because you disagree with an estimate or because a shop declined to change its price.

Email hello@quotegauge.example with the email address or order reference from your receipt. Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days.
