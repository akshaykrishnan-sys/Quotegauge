How QuoteGauge prices a quote

Every fair range is an estimate built from the standard labor time for the job, typical labor rates for your area and type of provider, and typical parts or materials cost from economy to premium. It is a starting point for a better conversation, not an inspection or a guarantee.

What goes into a range

  • Labor time. Car repairs are priced from the standard time for that job on that vehicle. Home and contractor work uses typical hours for the trade and the size of the job.
  • Labor rate. Typical hourly rates for your ZIP code and the kind of provider. Dealerships and licensed specialists usually charge more, and the range allows for that.
  • Parts and materials. From economy to original-equipment or premium quality, so a fair price for the parts you're getting stays inside the range.
  • Room for honest variation. On bigger jobs the top of each range is set at a price a reputable provider might honestly charge with quality parts.

How lines are marked

  • Fair: at or within 10% of the top of its range.
  • Above market: more than 10% over the top of its range.
  • Needs verification: it can't be judged without seeing the car or the job, the price is unreadable, or the description is too vague.
  • Questionable charge: a line that is often padded or sold as an upsell. It may still be right for you; it's worth a question.

What it can't know

  • The condition of your car or home, or what the technician found
  • The exact brand and grade of parts the provider plans to use
  • Rust, hidden damage, access problems and other things that add real labor

That is why some lines come back as needs verification, and why the report never claims a provider acted dishonestly. Use it to ask better questions: most honest shops and contractors welcome them.

What happens to your quote

Your quote is sent to our processing provider to be analysed and is not stored by QuoteGauge after the report is produced. No shop, dealership or contractor pays us, and we don't sell personal data.

When we get it wrong

Every report has a "Was this right?" button, and every flagged mistake is reviewed to improve future checks. If a single report wasn't useful, email hello@quotegauge.pro within 7 days for a full refund.
