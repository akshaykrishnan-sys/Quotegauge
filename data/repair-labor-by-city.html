Repair Labor Cost Index by City, 2026: Mechanic and HVAC Wages in 10 US Metros

HVAC technicians earn the most in Chicago ($38.14 an hour, +22% vs the US mean of $31.14) and the least in Tampa ($26.89). Auto technicians earn the most in Denver ($31.44) and the least in Tampa ($25.83), against a US mean of $27.13 (BLS OEWS, May 2025).

What it means for your quote

  • Labor is roughly half of a typical car repair bill and 30 to 50 percent of an HVAC or contractor job, so a metro with wages 15 percent above average should run about 6 to 8 percent above national prices overall.
  • Parts and equipment prices vary much less by city than labor does.
  • Shop and contractor hourly rates are well above technician wages because they cover overhead, but they move with local wages.

Method

We took the mean hourly wage for automotive service technicians and mechanics (SOC 49-3023) and for heating, air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics and installers (SOC 49-9021) in each metro area, and divided by the national mean. The city pages apply that ratio to the labor share of each national price range.
