About QuoteGauge

QuoteGauge is an online tool that checks car repair, home repair and contractor quotes against typical US prices, line by line, and tells you what to question before you approve the work. It is operated by Akshay Krishnan in Bengaluru, India, and serves customers in the United States.

Who runs it

QuoteGauge is run by Akshay Krishnan, Bengaluru, India. It is independent: it is not owned by, paid by or affiliated with any repair shop, dealership, contractor, parts seller or manufacturer, and no shop pays to be recommended. Contact: hello@quotegauge.pro. We usually reply within one business day. US mailing address: 123 Main St, STE 200 #456, San Francisco, CA 94105.

What it does

  • Reads a photo, PDF or typed copy of your estimate.
  • Prices each line against the typical range for that job on your vehicle or home, in your area.
  • Flags lines that look high, unclear or possibly unnecessary, and gives you questions and a short script to use with the shop.
  • Compares up to five quotes side by side.

How it prices a quote

Each check uses an language model (Anthropic's Claude) guided by standard repair labor times, typical US hourly labor rates by area and shop type, and typical parts prices. Results are estimates, not an inspection or a binding price. The full method, including what QuoteGauge cannot know, is on How we price. Our published cost guides list the outside sources behind each price range.

Plans and prices

  • Every check starts with a free first answer: the overall verdict.
  • A single full report costs $4.99 to $14.99 depending on the type of check.
  • Check Any Quote Unlimited costs $26 a month for every report on every quote. Cancel anytime.

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, which acts as our merchant of record and handles billing, receipts and sales tax.

Refunds

If a single full report was not useful, email us within 7 days and we refund it. Unlimited can be cancelled at any time. Full details: Refund policy.

Privacy

Quotes you upload are sent to our processing provider (Anthropic) only to produce your report, are not kept afterwards, and are never sold or shared with shops. Details: Privacy policy and Terms of use.

What QuoteGauge is not

It is not a mechanic, contractor, inspector, insurer or lender, and it cannot see your vehicle or property. For large or safety-critical repairs, get at least two itemized quotes and ask the shop to show you the worn or failed part.
