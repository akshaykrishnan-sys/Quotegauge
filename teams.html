Questions teams ask first.

Will this upset our vendors?

No vendor sees QuoteGauge. You get the numbers privately and decide what to ask. Most teams use it to ask better questions, not to switch vendors.

Where do the fair prices come from?

Local labor rates from U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics wage data for your metro, combined with typical parts and materials costs for each job. Every range shows its basis.

What trades do you cover?

HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, appliances, pest control, landscaping, turnovers and fleet vehicle repairs.

Is our data private?

Quotes are used only to produce your report. We never share them with vendors, owners or anyone else.

Can we cancel?

Yes. Plans are monthly and you can cancel anytime from the billing link in your receipt.
