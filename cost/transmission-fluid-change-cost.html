Transmission Fluid Change Cost in 2026: Drain-and-Fill vs Flush

A transmission drain-and-fill costs about $80 to $250 at a shop in 2026, and a machine flush costs about $150 to $400. A drain-and-fill replaces roughly a third of the fluid; a flush replaces nearly all of it. Use only the fluid your owner's manual specifies.

What a fair quote includes

  • Fluid of the exact type your manual specifies
  • Drain plug or pan service, and a filter if your transmission has one
  • Checking the fluid level by the correct procedure

What pushes the price up

  • Fluid type and capacity
  • Flush versus drain-and-fill
  • Filter and pan gasket
  • Dealer versus independent shop

Most US shops bill labor by the hour using a published labor-time guide, so the hours on your quote should be close to the standard time for your vehicle, even if the mechanic works faster.

Red flags on a quote

  • A flush sold far before your manual's interval
  • Generic 'multi-vehicle' fluid used where a specific fluid is required
  • A flush recommended on a high-mileage transmission with no service history (a gentler drain-and-fill is often safer)

Questions to ask before you approve

  • What interval does my manual give?
  • Which exact fluid will you use?
  • Is it a drain-and-fill or a flush, and how many quarts?

Common questions

Is a flush better than a drain-and-fill?

A flush replaces more fluid, but many shops prefer repeated drain-and-fills on older or neglected transmissions.

How often should transmission fluid be changed?

It varies widely, from 30,000 miles to the manufacturer's 'lifetime'. Follow your manual, and shorten it for towing.

Does fluid type matter?

Yes. Transmission fluids are not interchangeable; the wrong one can cause damage.
