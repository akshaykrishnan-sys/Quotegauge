Water Heater Replacement Cost in 2026: Tank vs Tankless

Replacing a standard tank water heater usually costs $900 to $2,500 installed in 2026; Angi's data averages about $1,346. Tankless units cost about $1,400 to $3,900 installed. Labor alone is usually $150 to $450 for a tank swap.

What a fair quote includes

  • New heater, connectors and a new relief valve
  • Removal and disposal of the old tank
  • Permit and code items such as an expansion tank or strapping where required

What pushes the price up

  • Tank versus tankless
  • Fuel type and venting
  • Code upgrades (expansion tank, seismic straps, drain pan)
  • Moving the heater to a new location

Red flags on a quote

  • Tankless pushed without checking gas line size and venting
  • Code items charged separately at high prices without explanation
  • A quote with no brand, capacity or warranty
  • Emergency pricing on a non-emergency install

Questions to ask before you approve

  • Brand, capacity, and the tank and parts warranty?
  • Which code items are required in my area, and what does each cost?
  • Is the permit and haul-away included?

Common questions

How long does a water heater last?

Tank units often last 8 to 12 years; tankless units can last longer.

Is tankless worth it?

It saves space and can save energy, but installation costs more, especially if gas lines or venting must change.

How long does replacement take?

A like-for-like tank swap is usually done in a few hours.
