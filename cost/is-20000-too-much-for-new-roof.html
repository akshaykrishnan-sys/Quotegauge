Is $20,000 Too Much for A new roof? (2026)

Usually yes. $20,000 for a new roof is above the typical 2026 US range for most versions of this job. It is high for typical asphalt roof, 1,500 to 2,000 sq ft, where the usual price is $8,000 – $16,000.

When $20,000 can be fair

  • Roof size, pitch and complexity
  • Shingle grade (3-tab, architectural, luxury)
  • Rotten decking that must be replaced
  • Number of layers being torn off

Signs $20,000 is padded

  • A lump sum with no square count
  • Decking replacement priced per sheet with no cap or estimate
  • Reusing old flashing without saying so
  • A storm-chaser asking you to sign over your insurance claim

What to ask before you pay

  • How many squares is my roof, and what is the price per square?
  • What is the price per sheet of decking, and what is your estimate of how much?
  • Which shingle line, and what are the manufacturer and workmanship warranties?

What is a normal price?

Replacing an asphalt shingle roof usually costs $8,000 to $16,000 in 2026, with a national average around $12,000 (Fixr). Per square foot, expect about $3 to $5 for 3-tab and $4 to $8 for architectural shingles, installed. Roofers usually quote per 'square' (100 square feet).
