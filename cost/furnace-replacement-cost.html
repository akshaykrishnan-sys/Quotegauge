Furnace Replacement Cost in 2026: Gas, Electric and Efficiency

A gas furnace replacement usually costs $3,800 to $10,000 installed in 2026, with a national average around $4,800 (Fixr). Electric furnaces cost about $1,800 to $7,300. High-efficiency (90%+ AFUE) condensing units cost more and may need new venting.

What a fair quote includes

  • New furnace sized for your home
  • Gas, electrical and venting connections
  • Permit, removal of the old unit, and startup and safety tests

What pushes the price up

  • Efficiency (AFUE) and two-stage or modulating burners
  • New venting or condensate drain for high-efficiency units
  • Size in BTU
  • Ductwork changes and local permits

Red flags on a quote

  • Furnace size copied from the old unit with no load calculation
  • No AFUE, BTU or model number listed
  • A 'cracked heat exchanger' claim with no photo or test result
  • Duct cleaning or air purifiers bundled in by default

Questions to ask before you approve

  • What BTU size, and how was it calculated?
  • Model number, AFUE and warranty terms?
  • If you found a cracked heat exchanger, can I see a photo or camera footage?

Common questions

How long does a furnace last?

Usually 15 to 20 years.

Is a high-efficiency furnace worth it?

In cold climates with long heating seasons, often yes; in mild climates the payback is slower.

Can a furnace be replaced in one day?

A straight swap usually takes one day.
