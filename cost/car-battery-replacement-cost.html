Car Battery Replacement Cost in 2026: Fair Installed Price

A standard 12-volt car battery usually costs $150 to $350 installed in 2026. Walmart lists common sizes at about $150 to $190, and many parts stores install for free. AGM batteries for start-stop cars cost more, often $200 to $400, and some cars need a battery registration step.

What a fair quote includes

  • New battery of the right group size and cold-cranking amps
  • Fitting, terminal cleaning and a charging test
  • Recycling the old battery (often refunds a core charge)

What pushes the price up

  • Battery type (AGM versus flooded)
  • Group size and brand
  • Battery location (under a seat or in the trunk takes longer)
  • Registration or coding on some European cars

Most US shops bill labor by the hour using a published labor-time guide, so the hours on your quote should be close to the standard time for your vehicle, even if the mechanic works faster.

Red flags on a quote

  • Battery replaced without a load test
  • Premium AGM sold for a car that only needs a standard battery
  • A charging system diagnosis charged when the battery simply tested bad

Questions to ask before you approve

  • What did the load test show?
  • Does my car require AGM?
  • Is there a core refund and an installation fee?

Common questions

How long does a car battery last?

Usually 3 to 5 years, less in hot climates.

Do I need an AGM battery?

Only if the car came with one, which is common on start-stop vehicles.

Can I install it myself?

On most cars, yes. Some newer cars need the battery registered with a scan tool.
