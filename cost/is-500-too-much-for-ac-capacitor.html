Is $500 Too Much for An AC capacitor? (2026)

It depends on the job. $500 for an AC capacitor is within the typical 2026 US range for after-hours or peak-season call. It is high for standard run capacitor, weekday, where the usual price is $150 – $250.

When $500 can be fair

  • Weekday versus emergency or weekend timing
  • Single versus dual-run capacitor
  • Contractor service-call minimums
  • Whether a hard-start kit is added

Signs $500 is padded

  • A capacitor quoted at $400+ on a weekday with no other work
  • A full system replacement pushed because of a failed capacitor
  • A hard-start kit added without explaining why

What to ask before you pay

  • What did the capacitor read versus its rating?
  • What is the service call fee, and what is the part price?
  • Did the compressor and fan test normal after?
