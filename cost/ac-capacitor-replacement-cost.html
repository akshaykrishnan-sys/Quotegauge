AC Capacitor Replacement Cost in 2026: Fair Price for a Service Call

An AC capacitor replacement usually costs $150 to $400 in 2026, including the service call, diagnosis, part and labor. The capacitor itself is typically $15 to $75 wholesale. This Old House puts the average service call around $129. Nights, weekends and peak summer can push it to $250 to $600.

Typical ranges from the published sources below, checked 2026-11-19. Taxes, shop fees and your local labor rate can move the total.

What a fair quote includes

  • Service call and diagnosis
  • Correctly rated capacitor (microfarads and voltage)
  • Testing the compressor and fan motors after

What pushes the price up

  • Weekday versus emergency or weekend timing
  • Single versus dual-run capacitor
  • Contractor service-call minimums
  • Whether a hard-start kit is added

Red flags on a quote

  • A capacitor quoted at $400+ on a weekday with no other work
  • A full system replacement pushed because of a failed capacitor
  • A hard-start kit added without explaining why

Questions to ask before you approve

  • What did the capacitor read versus its rating?
  • What is the service call fee, and what is the part price?
  • Did the compressor and fan test normal after?

Common questions

What does a bad capacitor do?

The outdoor unit hums but the fan or compressor will not start, or the AC cuts in and out.

Is a capacitor a big repair?

No. It is one of the cheapest common AC repairs.

Can I replace it myself?

Capacitors hold a dangerous charge even when power is off. Most homeowners should use a technician.
