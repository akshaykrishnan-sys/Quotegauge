Spark Plug Replacement Cost in 2026: 4-Cylinder vs V6

Replacing spark plugs costs about $100 to $250 on a four-cylinder engine and about $165 to $400 on a V6 at an independent shop in 2026, with iridium plugs. V8s and engines with buried rear plugs cost more because the intake may need to come off.

Typical ranges from the published sources below, checked 2026-11-19. Taxes, shop fees and your local labor rate can move the total.

What a fair quote includes

  • New plugs of the type your manual specifies
  • Labor to remove coils or wires and fit the plugs
  • A check of the coils and boots

What pushes the price up

  • Number of cylinders
  • Plug access: some V6 engines need the intake plenum removed
  • Plug type (iridium versus platinum)
  • Coils replaced at the same time

Most US shops bill labor by the hour using a published labor-time guide, so the hours on your quote should be close to the standard time for your vehicle, even if the mechanic works faster.

Red flags on a quote

  • Plugs replaced far before the interval in your manual
  • All ignition coils replaced without a misfire code or test
  • A 'tune-up' package with fuel-system cleaning bundled in

Questions to ask before you approve

  • What is the replacement interval for my engine?
  • Does my engine need the intake removed?
  • Did any coil test bad, or is the coil replacement optional?

Common questions

How often should spark plugs be replaced?

Many modern iridium plugs last 60,000 to 120,000 miles. Check your owner's manual.

Why does my V6 cost so much more?

On many transverse V6 engines the rear plugs sit under the intake, which adds labor.

Should I replace coils too?

Only if they test bad or are causing misfires.
