Serpentine Belt Replacement Cost in 2026: Fair Price

A serpentine belt replacement usually costs $100 to $250 in 2026. RepairPal's national estimate is about $146 to $203, with labor around $85 to $124. Adding a new tensioner raises it to about $150 to $400.

What a fair quote includes

  • New belt routed to the factory diagram
  • A check of the tensioner and pulleys
  • A noise and charging check

What pushes the price up

  • Belt access (some engines need covers or mounts removed)
  • Tensioner or idler pulleys added
  • Vehicles with more than one drive belt

Most US shops bill labor by the hour using a published labor-time guide, so the hours on your quote should be close to the standard time for your vehicle, even if the mechanic works faster.

Red flags on a quote

  • Belt plus tensioner plus every pulley quoted with no sign of wear
  • More than 1.5 hours of labor for a belt on a common car
  • A belt quoted alongside a timing belt as if they were the same part

Questions to ask before you approve

  • Is the tensioner actually worn (noise, wobble, weak spring)?
  • What is the labor time from the guide?
  • Is this the serpentine belt, not the timing belt?

Common questions

How often should the serpentine belt be changed?

Often every 60,000 to 100,000 miles, or when it cracks, frays or squeals.

What happens if it breaks?

You can lose charging, power steering and, on many engines, the water pump, so the engine can overheat.

Is it the same as a timing belt?

No. The serpentine belt drives accessories outside the engine; the timing belt is inside and costs far more to replace.
