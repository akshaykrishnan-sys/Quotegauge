Central AC Replacement Cost in 2026: What Is a Fair Quote

Replacing a central AC unit on existing ductwork usually costs about $4,000 to $9,500 installed in 2026. Trane puts the national range at $3,900 for a basic unit to $13,500 at the high end. Size (tons), efficiency (SEER2) and whether the indoor coil is replaced drive most of the difference.

What a fair quote includes

  • Outdoor condenser and matching indoor coil
  • Refrigerant line work, electrical disconnect and pad
  • Permit, removal of the old unit, and startup testing

What pushes the price up

  • System size in tons (should come from a load calculation, not just the old unit)
  • SEER2 efficiency and brand
  • Ductwork or electrical upgrades
  • Local labor rates and permits

Red flags on a quote

  • A size chosen without any load calculation
  • No brand, model number or SEER2 rating on the quote
  • Ductwork replacement added without an inspection showing a problem
  • Pressure to sign the same day for a 'discount'

Questions to ask before you approve

  • What size is this, and how was it calculated?
  • Exact brand, model numbers and SEER2 for the outdoor unit and coil?
  • What do the warranties cover (parts and labor), and is the permit included?

Common questions

How long does a central AC last?

Often 15 to 20 years with maintenance.

Repair or replace?

A common rule: if the unit's age times the repair cost exceeds about $5,000, replacement usually makes more sense.

Do I need to replace the indoor coil too?

Usually yes, so the system is matched and works with the newer refrigerant.
