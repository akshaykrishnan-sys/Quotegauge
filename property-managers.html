Who is QuoteGauge for?

Property managers, landlords with 10 or more units, facilities teams and small fleets that approve vendor quotes every week for HVAC, plumbing, roofing, appliances, turnovers or vehicle repairs.

How much does QuoteGauge for Teams cost?

The Team plan is $199 a month for up to 5 people and 150 quote checks a month. The Portfolio plan is $499 a month for unlimited people and 600 checks a month. The first 10 teams get 50% off for their first 6 months.

Does QuoteGauge replace our vendors?

No. It checks the quotes they send, so you approve fair ones faster and push back on padded lines with specific figures. Most vendors are honest; the check is about consistency, not accusation.
