What you give us

When you check a quote you provide your vehicle's year, make and model, a ZIP code, the type of repair shop, and the quote itself as a photo or typed text. You do not need an account.

How it is used

These details are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, solely to produce your report. QuoteGauge does not keep your quote or photo after the report is produced, and does not use it for advertising.

Payments

If you buy a full report, payment is handled by our payment provider, [payment provider]. QuoteGauge never sees or stores your full card number. We receive a confirmation of payment and basic order details.

What your browser stores

Your browser remembers the vehicle and ZIP code you last entered so you don't have to retype them. This stays on your device and you can clear it in your browser settings.

Logs

Our hosting provider records standard technical logs, such as IP address and time of request, to keep the service secure and working. These are kept for a limited period.

Selling data

QuoteGauge does not sell personal data and does not share it with repair shops, dealerships or advertisers.

Your choices

To ask what information we hold about you, or to ask for it to be deleted, email hello@quotegauge.example.

Changes
